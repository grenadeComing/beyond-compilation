Each statement in `benchmark/benchmark.jsonl` reproduces a statement from an openly licensed textbook and keeps that textbook's license. The `source`, `source_repo`, `source_commit`, `source_line` and `source_license` fields record where it comes from.

When you redistribute or adapt the statements, give credit to the original authors and follow the license of each source.

## Model outputs, grades and human scores

The files in `outputs/`, `human_audits/` and `checks/` contain Lean formalizations generated by the evaluated systems, judge grades and explanations, and anonymized human scores. Each Lean output is a formalization of a benchmark statement, so redistribute it under the license of that statement's source. Use of each system's outputs is also subject to that system's terms.
